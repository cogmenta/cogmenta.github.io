Pitch animations

The homepage motion, rendered as 1600×900 loops for the deck. GIF for Google Slides, MP4 (H.264) for Keynote and PowerPoint — same frames, much smaller and sharper. Each card also links to the live animation page it was rendered from. Source: cogmenta.tech.

Re-render after a change with node pitch/render.mjs (all) or node pitch/render.mjs rsi-light (one). Needs Chrome and ffmpeg. The pages under pitch/anim/ take ?theme=dark, ?legend=1 and ?domain=ip|ma|med; without ?seek=1 they play on their own.
